BEM-DRAW

BEM-DRAW is a TapeOut.space raffle application on X Layer, offering member-only draws and BEM-paid public rounds with prizes such as TapeOut miners, BEM, and physical goods. The member draw page displays on-chain entry lists and results, describes block-hash randomness with TapeOut circuit-based number selection, and provides a browser replay for verification. Public rounds publish their funding targets, refund conditions, platform fees, and fund-allocation rules.

PROJECT 02Active

CoTape

CoTape is a chip-design and materials collaboration platform on BNB Chain. Designers publish fully verified netlists, while backers pool manufacturing materials in NAND shares. Fabrication follows full funding and the designer’s collateral deposit, with mining rigs held in contract custody. Base mining rewards go to backers; the optimal-design bonus is split according to the listing after platform fees.

PROJECT 03Active

EmberWorld

EmberWorld is a DeWEB action-adventure game on X Layer, built around circuit-controlled teammates. Its design reuses TapeOut ecosystem mining-rig netlists, initially focusing on FabX, as tactical brains for cooperative combat. The planned model keeps manual play free, uses an EmberWorld Pass NFT to unlock circuit collaboration, and includes NFT equipment trading. The website shows combat demos from the earlier Ember game; the full EmberWorld, mining-rig integration, and item shop remain in development.

PROJECT 04Active

Fab X

Fab X is a TapeOut community mining platform on X Layer. Users assemble NAND and LATCH components into circuits, fabricate circuit NFTs, and validate solutions to tasks to participate in FAB mining. Its website provides a circuit canvas with AI-assisted design, transistor and circuit markets, DeWEB tools, and on-chain statistics. Mining weight accounts for fabrication cost and design efficiency; the site also describes boosts linked to DeWEB activity and BEM or OKB staking. Protocol and marketplace rules are executed on X Layer.

Its tools also cover circuit fabrication, containers, mining, and portfolio management. The site describes itself as non-custodial, with transactions authorized through the user’s wallet.

PROJECT 07Active

SnapVault

SnapVault is a creator content-sharing app on X Layer. Creators publish encrypted images, and fans pay OKB to unlock them; it also offers private chats and fan groups. Its frontend is hosted on DeWEB, updated through TapeCode, and accessed through TapeKit. When a compatible container and public key are available, TapeSend delivers viewing links; otherwise, the app uses standard delivery. Timed access limits in-app viewing but cannot prevent screenshots or revoke saved content. Delivery issues can be submitted for platform refund review.

PROJECT 09Active

TapeAPI

TapeAPI is TapeOut’s signed API layer. It adds a signing sidecar in front of OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible endpoints and attaches a verifiable receipt to each answer, binding the request, response, usage, and price claims. Its SDK can check the receipt against on-chain records; the site currently marks it as 2.x, MIT code, and a CC0 specification.

PROJECT 15Active

TapeOut Community Foundation

TapeOut Community Foundation is an independent, community-led initiative supporting builders in the TapeOut ecosystem. It plans to fund public goods, shared tools, developer education, research, and security audits, with publicly verifiable contributions recorded through non-transferable Proof of Build (PoB). PROJECT 20Active

TapePool

TapePool is a lending pool for X Layer miners. PROJECT 22Active

TapeSurge

TapeSurge is an on-chain yield-distribution experiment built on TapeOut PoD on BNB Smart Chain. Its design pools BEM to buy and hold PoD mining rigs. Standard rounds accumulate output for successive draws; monthly rounds mine for 30 days, splitting the prize pool between proportional payouts and draws using Chainlink VRF randomness. It is currently in testnet development, with mainnet participation not yet open and no fixed returns or completion times promised.

TapeUP

TapeUP describes itself as a fully on-chain ledger protocol built on DeWEB. It reads identity, page, and message records from their source chains, verifies them locally, then combines them into one cross-chain view. Reading and verification are client-side; TapeUP does not execute transactions on destination chains.

PROJECT 24Active

tapeXO

tapeXO is an X/Twitter raffle tool built on TapeOut and deployed on X Layer. It supports X-login registration or importing repost lists from existing posts, without wallet connections or signatures for participation. Entrant lists are frozen and hashed on chain before the draw; a commit-reveal process combines a seed with a later block hash to produce a reproducible ranking. Public entrant and result files are archived in TapeOut containers, with hashes recorded on chain for verification. Optional eligibility checks include follows, reposts, and token or NFT holdings. The operator submits transactions and pays gas.

Visit tapeXO ↗tapeXO on X ↗
PROJECT 25Active

ToshX

ToshX is an IGO (Initial Genesis Offering) launch and trading terminal for verified agents on BNB Smart Chain. Its current rules restrict GENESIS purchases to Binance Agent ID holders with identity-based limits. Purchases execute on a public curve from P to 1.5P and are non-refundable; 20% of purchase funds go to the project and 80% to the opening pool. The website also describes a pre-opening order market, opening at 2P with permanently locked liquidity, and 4,000 fixed-price shelves for subsequent sales. Project applications require an existing product or verifiable work.
