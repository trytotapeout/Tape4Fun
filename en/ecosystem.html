PROJECT 01Active

ToshX

ToshX (Tosh Protocol) is a fair-launch and trading terminal for the BEM ecosystem on BNB Smart Chain. According to its website, participation limits depend on a wallet’s historical gas expenditure, launches have no premine or reserved team allocation, and each project gets a dedicated PancakeSwap Infinity pool. After GENESIS fundraising, remaining supply trades through 4,000 fixed price shelves; contracts enforce deposit limits, fundraising, and refund rules.

PROJECT 02Active

TapeAPI

TapeAPI is TapeOut’s signed API layer. It adds a signing sidecar in front of OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible endpoints and attaches a verifiable receipt to each answer, binding the request, response, usage, and price claims. Its SDK can check the receipt against on-chain records; the site currently marks it as 2.x, MIT code, and a CC0 specification.

Visit TapeAPI ↗
PROJECT 03Active

TapeUP

TapeUP describes itself as a fully on-chain ledger protocol built on DeWEB. It reads identity, page, and message records from their source chains, verifies them locally, then combines them into one cross-chain view. Reading and verification are client-side; TapeUP does not execute transactions on destination chains.

PROJECT 04Active

TapeAI

TapeAI is building an Agent ecosystem where circuits and containers carry identity, decision logic, and authorization boundaries. Its current Inspector circuit flow grants one on-chain World Constitution signing right per circuit. Agent creation, asset review, and protocol-fee distribution are later-stage mechanisms, not currently open features.

TapeLiquid

TapeLiquid presents itself as the first TapeOut DEX on X Layer, combining spot and perpetual markets with an on-chain frontend. Its profile says trading fees burn BEM while users mine TAPE.

PROJECT 06Active

TapeFoundry

TapeFoundry is a design house building a computer one circuit at a time on TapeOut. Its first Arithmetic series offers 11 real gate-level designs as 3,450 numbered on-chain cards; public netlists make each circuit inspectable, and cards are priced by the gates burned to fabricate them.

PROJECT 07Active

TapeDeck

TapeDeck is a desktop app for safely browsing and publishing decentralized sites. It verifies on-chain site files, checks sites and explains signing risks before a wallet action, supports site-specific wallet permissions, and publishes a local folder directly on chain.

Visit TapeDeck ↗
PROJECT 08Active

TapePool

TapePool is a lending pool for X Layer miners. Miners can borrow against the BEM their TapeOut or FabX rigs are expected to mine, with a Rig Agent holding the voucher and using harvests to repay automatically. Loans are denominated in BEM with fixed 7- or 14-day terms, no oracle, and no liquidation from price moves; lenders deposit BEM and receive tpBEM representing their pool share.

Visit TapePool ↗TapePool on X ↗
PROJECT 09Active

CoTape

CoTape is a chip-design and materials collaboration platform on BNB Chain. Designers publish fully verified netlists, while backers pool manufacturing materials in NAND shares. Fabrication follows full funding and the designer’s collateral deposit, with mining rigs held in contract custody. Base mining rewards go to backers; the optimal-design bonus is split according to the listing after platform fees.

Visit CoTape ↗CoTape on X ↗
PROJECT 10Active

TapeSurge

TapeSurge is an on-chain yield-distribution experiment built on TapeOut PoD on BNB Smart Chain. Its design pools BEM to buy and hold PoD mining rigs. Standard rounds accumulate output for successive draws; monthly rounds mine for 30 days, splitting the prize pool between proportional payouts and draws using Chainlink VRF randomness. It is currently in testnet development, with mainnet participation not yet open and no fixed returns or completion times promised.

Visit TapeSurge ↗TapeSurge on X ↗
